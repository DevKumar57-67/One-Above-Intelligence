## 🔬 About One Above Research

**One Above Research** is an independent technology research initiative focused on exploring the intersection of:

> **Artificial Intelligence × Quantum Computing × Cybersecurity × Computer Science**

The goal is not simply to consume existing technologies, but to **understand them from first principles, experiment with them, build practical systems, and contribute useful open-source work.**

This repository serves as the central research workspace for experiments, implementations, simulations, datasets, prototypes, technical notes, and research projects developed under One Above Research.

---

# 🧠 Research Philosophy

One Above Research follows a simple philosophy:

### **Understand → Question → Experiment → Build → Measure → Share**

We prioritize:

**First Principles**

Understand the underlying mathematics, algorithms, architectures, and assumptions before relying on abstractions.

**Experimentation**

Turn ideas into experiments and prototypes.

**Reproducibility**

Research should be understandable and reproducible by others whenever possible.

**Open Source**

Useful implementations, tools, and educational resources should be shared with the community.

**Interdisciplinary Thinking**

Interesting problems often exist between disciplines rather than inside a single field.

# 📚 Research Methodology

Each substantial research project should ideally contain:

```text
01. Problem Definition
        ↓
02. Background Research
        ↓
03. Existing Approaches
        ↓
04. Hypothesis / Research Question
        ↓
05. Mathematical / Technical Foundation
        ↓
06. Implementation
        ↓
07. Experiments
        ↓
08. Evaluation
        ↓
09. Results
        ↓
10. Limitations
        ↓
11. Future Work
        ↓
12. Open Source
```

This structure helps separate **what we know**, **what we hypothesize**, **what we implemented**, and **what the experiments actually demonstrate**.
